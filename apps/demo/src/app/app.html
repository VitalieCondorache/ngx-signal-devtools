How it works

  1. provideSignalDevtools() installs a registry — only in development.
  2. devSignal, devComputed and devEffect wrap the Angular primitives and record activity.
  3. trackSignal observes signals you already have, without changing their identity.
  4. Dependencies and live consumers are read from the private reactive graph through ɵSIGNAL, with a graceful fallback when it is unavailable.
  5. The overlay is a lazy chunk: production builds never download it, and devSignal degrades to a single reference check.
